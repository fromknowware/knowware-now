What the colophon is.

The colophon is a tradition in printed books — the closing page that names the people who made it possible. Knowware: Volume I will have one.

It lists every pre-order holder and every contributor, in one list. One typeface, one size, no ranks, no job titles. Your name appears exactly as you gave it.

After December 21, 2026, the list is sealed permanently. The name list is final at print.

What it is not.

Not a backer tier. Not a thank-you credit with different levels of size or placement. There is no "founding member" badge, no special colour, no early-access tier.

The colophon is not the acknowledgments section either — that is reserved for manuscript contributors (Path D editors) who return line-level notes.

One list. You are either on it, or you are not.
