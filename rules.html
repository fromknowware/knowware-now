Five rules this project will not break.

These are not guidelines. They are the conditions under which Knowware exists. If any is violated, the project is something else.

01
No tracking. No analytics. No pixels.

There is no tracking script on this site. No analytics, no cookies, no third-party share pixels. Sharing is navigator.share and clipboard copy only. We do not know how many people visit. We do not want to.

If you want to tell us something reached you, send a letter. That is the only signal we are collecting.

02
Blue is earned. Never decoration.

The signal colour — this blue — appears only on thesis words, the one line that matters on a given page, and action links. It is never used to decorate, separate, or draw attention to something that does not deserve it.

When everything is blue, nothing is. We use it once per screen.

03
No runtime AI features. Synthesis is in the manuscript.

There are no chatbots, no generative summaries, no AI-in-the-browser features on this site. The intelligence in this project lives in the manuscript — produced over three years by a method developed for the purpose.

The synthesis happened before you arrived. You are reading the result. Read the method →

04
The corpus is not the person. Never "X says."

Each of the 81 syntheses is built from a corpus — published work, lectures, interviews, and field notes attributed to a person. The corpus surfaces what the conversation produced.

We never write "Pearl says" or "Hayles argues." We write "the corpus surfaces" or "the synthesis finds." The distinction matters. A person has a right to speak for themselves. A corpus is a record, not a voice.

05
Machine proposes. Human approves every drop before fire.

This book is machine-coordinated. The production method uses language models at every stage — drafting, synthesis, cross-referencing, and structural analysis.

Every line that enters the manuscript has been read, approved, and edited by a human. Nothing fires automatically. Nothing publishes without a decision. Machine proposes; human approves. That is the rule. It does not bend.
